Pathly gives every student a clear view of their case, and gives the institute's team one place to work. Choose an interface below. All names, dates and documents are fictional.

Desktop-first. Review documents, approve or reject with a reason and a new deadline, and see who needs a call. Try the review queue.

Design system

Tokens, a nine-state status system with a separate deadline marker, and the components behind both interfaces. Built to WCAG 2.2 AA.

Open design system

The problem it solves

  • Case information was scattered across spreadsheets, a task board and email.
  • Clients could not see their status, so they phoned for updates.
  • Wrong or outdated documents led to long chains of reminders and calls.

Fictional demo data. The upload and review actions are simulated and send nothing anywhere.

The visual style was inspired by a soft dashboard reference; the layouts, status system and components are original work.
